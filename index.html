Peqi'in Cave

A digital spatial database of a Late Chalcolithic burial site, Upper Galilee, Israel

About this project

This is an interactive digital map of Peqi'in Cave, a major Late Chalcolithic burial site excavated in the Upper Galilee. Every area, excavation square, and stratigraphic locus in the cave is digitized as a clickable region, linked directly to the artifacts recorded there — ossuaries, flint tools, pottery, beads, and more.

The goal is to make it possible to actually ask spatial questions of the excavation data: does a given artifact type cluster in particular areas or loci of the cave? Do certain find types consistently co-occur in the same contexts? The underlying database is structured specifically to support that kind of analysis, not just to catalog objects.

Built as a final assignment for a course on Chalcolithic burial sites, based on the excavation monograph Peqi'in: A Late Chalcolithic Burial Site, Upper Galilee, Israel (Shalem, Gal & Smithline).
